## 5. Health, safety and compliance disclaimer

The skills are **informational instructions for AI assistants**, not engineering, legal or
safety advice. Construction is a regulated activity:

- Estimates, structural decisions, safety-critical actions and contract-signing must be
  reviewed and approved by qualified human professionals.
- Safety-compliance and computer-vision skills do NOT replace statutory site safety systems;
  keep human oversight as required by law (including the EU AI Act, Regulation (EU) 2024/1689,
  where applicable).
- Always verify prices, quantities and classifications against official sources before
  binding use.
